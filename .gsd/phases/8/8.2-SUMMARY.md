# Phase 8.2 Summary: Landscape Optimization & Pinch-to-Zoom

## Objective Accomplished
Solved mobile gallery usability issues by implementing native pinch-to-zoom and pan functionality, and ensuring landscape orientation allows for a more immersive viewing experience.

## Changes Implemented
### `src/components/coastal/Lightbox.tsx`
- **Pinch-to-Zoom Gesture**: Added support for multi-touch pinch to scale images up to 4x.
- **Fluid Panning**: When zoomed, the image becomes draggable in all directions, allowing users to explore high-resolution details.
- **Double-Tap Shortcut**: Implemented a quick-zoom feature that toggles between 1x and 2.5x on double tap.
- **Intelligent Gesture Switching**: The carousel automatically switches between "Swipe to Paginate" (when scale = 1) and "Free Pan" (when zoomed), preventing accidental image changes while zooming.
- **Smooth Animations**: Leveraged `useMotionValue` and `useSpring` for lag-free scaling and panning interactions.
- **Orientation Fix**: By allowing users to zoom and pan, images that appear small in landscape due to aspect ratio constraints can now be easily enlarged to fill the screen width.

## Verification Results
- **Gestures**: Verified pinch detection and distance calculation logic.
- **Interactivity**: Verified that `drag` constraints are removed when zoomed, enabling free movement.
- **State Management**: Verified that zoom resets when moving to the next/previous image.
