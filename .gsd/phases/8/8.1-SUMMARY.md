# Phase 8.1 Summary: Immersive Lightbox Scaling & Mobile Optimization

## Objective Accomplished
Enhanced the gallery lightbox for mobile devices, ensuring photos occupy the maximum available screen space with a premium, edge-to-edge aesthetic.

## Changes Implemented
### `src/components/coastal/Lightbox.tsx`
- **Edge-to-Edge Layout**: Removed `p-4` padding and `max-w-6xl` constraints on mobile viewports, allowing images to touch the screen boundaries.
- **Deep Immersion**: Increased background opacity to `black/95` and `backdrop-blur-2xl` for a more focused, premium experience.
- **UI Compactness**: 
    - Reduced Top Bar padding and counter font size on mobile.
    - Repositioned the caption (`bottom-8`) and swipe hint (`bottom-2`) to minimize intrusion on the image.
- **Entrance Animation**: Refined the initial `motion` transition for a subtler, more elegant entrance (`scale: 0.98`, `y: 10`).

## Verification Results
- **Layout**: Verified that mobile-specific classes (`sm:`) preserve desktop layout while enabling the new immersive experience on small screens.
- **Aesthetics**: The darker backdrop and higher blur align with the "Editorial Coastal" design system.
