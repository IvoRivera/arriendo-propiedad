# Plan 9.2 Summary: Consultative Request Flow & Validations

Expanded the request modal to support long-stay consultative leads with a dedicated form structure.

## Changes:
- **src/components/coastal/CoastalRequestModal.tsx**:
    - **Schema Expansion**: Added `estimated_start_date`, `estimated_duration`, `stay_type`, `budget`, and `amenities` to the Zod schema.
    - **Conditional UI**: Implemented a switch in the modal body that toggles between standard date selection and the new long-stay fields.
    - **Amenities Chips**: Created a tactile, multi-select grid for selecting specific needs (Internet, Desk, etc.) using chip-style buttons.
    - **Dynamic Copy**: Updated headers, placeholders, and the submit button to reflect the user's intent (Standard vs. Long Stay).
    - **Responsive Layout**: Ensured the expanded form remains stable and usable on mobile devices.

## Verification:
- Opening the modal from the "Estadía prolongada" tab shows the consultative form.
- Opening it from the "Reserva por fechas" tab (or Hero/Footer) shows the standard booking form.
- Amenities chips toggle correctly and update the form state.
- Submit button text changes dynamically.
